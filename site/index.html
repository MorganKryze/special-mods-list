    What this is

    A catalogue of … Fallout 4 mods, each checked by hand against the current game version (runtime 1.11.240), Lunar Fallout Overhaul and Sim Settlements 2. It builds a list for you: every mod brings the mods it needs and the patches it shares with the rest, and the list warns you about conflicts.

    How mods are checked

    Each row records whether the author supports 1.11.240, whether it needs an F4SE DLL, what it does to precombines, its cost, how it gets on with Lunar and Sim Settlements 2, whether it locks a faction ending, and what it needs, patches or conflicts with. Every verdict links to its sources: changelogs, author posts, compatibility reports.

    Mods marked avoid stay in the catalogue with the reason, so you know why they are left out.

    Cost ratings

    Costs on a Mac were measured on a base M3 MacBook Air (16 GB, under Wine). Costs on a gaming PC are estimated, not measured yet: … mods so far, and mods rated low on the M3 count as low on the PC. Switch the machine in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

    Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Bethesda Softworks. Mod names, descriptions and images belong to their authors and come from Nexus Mods.
